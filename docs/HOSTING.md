# Putting SafeTab online (Render + Neon)

You'll end up with SafeTab at an address like `https://safetab.onrender.com`, with a database online. It updates automatically whenever a change is published.

- **Neon** hosts the database. Its free plan didn't require a card when this guide was written.
- **Render** runs the app. Its free plan sleeps after about 15 minutes without visitors, and the next visit takes about a minute to wake it. Upgrade to Render's paid plan before real customers rely on it.

Check both services' current plans and terms when you sign up.

**Your local data doesn't move online.** The hosted SafeTab starts empty, so create your account again there.

---

## Step 1: Database (Neon), about 5 minutes
1. Go to **neon.tech** and sign up. "Continue with GitHub" is easiest.
2. **Create project**:
   - Name: `safetab`
   - Postgres version: the default
   - Region: the closest to Ghana, e.g. **Europe (Frankfurt)**
3. On the project dashboard, open **Connect**. Copy the **connection string**. It looks like `postgresql://neondb_owner:…@ep-….neon.tech/neondb?sslmode=require`.
   Keep it private: it's the key to your database.

## Step 2: The app (Render), about 10 minutes
1. Go to **render.com** and sign up with **GitHub**. When asked, allow Render to access the **Dantech12/SafeTab** repository.
2. Click **New → Blueprint**, choose **Dantech12/SafeTab**, and select branch **`claude/vibrant-pasteur-oiptqy`**. Render reads `render.yaml` and shows a **safetab** web service.
3. Render asks for values. Fill them in:

   | Setting | What to enter |
   |---|---|
   | `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` | `https://safetab.onrender.com`. If Render gives your service a different address, update this afterwards. |
   | `DATABASE_OWNER_URL` | The Neon connection string from Step 1 |
   | `PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY` | Your Paystack **test** secret key (`sk_test_…`) |
   | `GOOGLE_CLOUD_PROJECT_ID` | `silent-snow-471809-m2` |
   | `GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON` | Open your service-account key file in Notepad, copy **everything**, and paste it |
   | `SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT`, `SMTP_SECURE`, `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASS`, `MAIL_FROM` | The same values as in your PC's `.env` (from `npm run set-email`) |
   | `GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_ID` / `_SECRET` | Same as your `.env`, or leave empty for now |
   | `SUPPORT_EMAIL`, `BUSINESS_ADDRESS` | Your details |

   `TOKEN_PEPPER` and `APP_DB_PASSWORD` are generated by Render automatically. Don't change them later: changing `TOKEN_PEPPER` signs everyone out.
4. Click **Apply**. The first build takes a few minutes. When the log shows `Server listening`, open your address. You should see the SafeTab product page.

## Step 3: Connect the services to your new address, about 10 minutes
Replace `https://safetab.onrender.com` below with your actual address.
- **Paystack:** Dashboard → Settings → **API Keys & Webhooks** → **Test Webhook URL** = `https://safetab.onrender.com/webhooks/paystack`.
- **Google sign-in** (if used): Google Cloud Console → your OAuth client → **Authorized redirect URIs** → add `https://safetab.onrender.com/auth/google/callback`.
- **Android Enterprise:** nothing to do. With an `https://` address, Google returns straight to SafeTab after signup, so there's no code to paste.

## Step 4: Check it works
1. Open your address and click **Choose Organization**. Pay with a Paystack **test card**, then create your account.
2. On the sign-in page, use **Forgot password?** with your own email. The email should arrive.
3. Invite yourself as a parent from a student's page. The invitation should arrive by email.

## How it differs from your PC
- **No "Activate pilot" button.** Online, a plan is only activated by a payment. Use test cards while in test mode.
- **Invitations go by email only.** For safety, the link isn't shown on screen online.
- **Updates go live automatically.** When I publish a change, Render redeploys it. You can watch this under **Events** in Render.

## Later
- **Your own domain** (e.g. `safetab.com.gh`): buy the domain, add it in Render under Settings → **Custom Domains**, then update `PUBLIC_BASE_URL`, the Paystack webhook and the Google redirect URI.
- **Going live with payments:** see `docs/GOING_LIVE.md`. Swap in your live Paystack key only once the business is registered and tablets can be enrolled.
- **Backups:** check Neon's backup and restore options for your plan.
