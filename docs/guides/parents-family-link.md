# Guide for parents: managing your child's tablet with Google Family Link

*Free · no factory reset · you stay in control*

The school asks families to keep **TikTok, games and other distracting apps off during school hours**. Because the tablet belongs to your family, **you** set this up, not the school. Google's free **Family Link** app lets you do it from your own phone.

> Menu names can differ slightly between Android versions and app updates. If something below doesn't match your screen, open Family Link's own help (**Menu → Help**).

## What you need
- **Your phone** (Android or iPhone), with the free **Google Family Link** app from the Play Store or App Store.
- **Your child's tablet**, connected to the internet.
- **A Google Account for your child.**
  - Under 13: create it inside Family Link.
  - 13 or older: the child signs in with their own account and agrees to supervision.
- About **15 minutes**.

## Step 1: Link the tablet (once)
1. On **your phone**, open **Family Link** and follow the steps to add your child.
2. On **the tablet**, open **Settings → Google → Parental controls** (on some tablets: **Settings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls**) and follow the steps. Sign in with your child's account when asked.
3. When both screens say setup is complete, your child's name appears in Family Link on your phone.

No reset is needed, and photos, files and apps stay on the tablet.

## Step 2: Turn off distracting apps during school hours
In Family Link, tap your child, then **Controls**.

**Option A: a school-time schedule** (recommended if you see it)
- Look for **School time** or **Downtime** under **Controls**. Set **Monday–Friday** with the school's hours, e.g. **07:30–15:00**.
- Some versions let you choose which apps still work during that time. Allow only school apps, such as the browser for schoolwork, calculator and learning apps.

**Option B: block specific apps**
- Go to **Controls → App limits**, find **TikTok** (and any games) and choose **Block**.
- Blocked apps stay installed with their data. Unblock them in the evening or at weekends if you wish.

**Option C: daily time limits**
- In **App limits**, choose **Set limit** for an app, e.g. 30 minutes a day.

## Step 3: Check it worked
On the tablet during the set hours, try opening TikTok. It should show that it's paused or blocked by your parent.

## Good to know
- **Your child can see** that the tablet is supervised. Family Link is open about this, and we encourage you to explain why to your child.
- **Older children:** once a child reaches the age of digital consent in your country (often 13), they may be able to turn off supervision. You'll be notified if that happens. Talking about the rules works better than relying only on settings.
- **Privacy:** Family Link is between you and Google. **The school does not see your child's tablet, apps or activity.**
- **Help:** [families.google/familylink](https://families.google/familylink/) and the Help section in the app.
