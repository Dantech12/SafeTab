Features

Everything a school needs. Nothing it shouldn't have.

Simple controls that keep tablets for learning — designed around children's privacy from day one.

School-hours rules

Pick the days, times and apps. Apps are switched off during lessons and come back automatically after school — never deleted.

Parent consent, built in

For family-owned tablets, parents read the exact rules and agree in their own login. Change a rule and it pauses until they agree again.

Honest status

See each tablet as pending, applied or failed. SafeTab never says a rule is on until the tablet confirms it.

Tablet management

Enroll with a QR code, lock a lost tablet, and retire tablets at the end of the year from one place.

Activity log

Every sensitive action is recorded — including who viewed a student's information — so staff stay accountable.

Transparent by design

A clear "what we collect" page for staff, parents and students. The same facts, in plain language, for everyone.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can the school see what students do on their tablets?

No. SafeTab shows whether a tablet is enrolled and whether rules are applied. It never shows messages, photos, screens, location, browsing history or which apps a student uses.

Our students' tablets belong to their parents. Can we still use SafeTab?

Yes, with consent. Parents review the exact school-hours rules and agree in their own login. Only those rules apply to family-owned tablets, and parents can withdraw at any time.

Do tablets need to be reset?

Yes, once. Android only allows full management to be set up when a tablet is first set up, so nobody can secretly take over a device in use. Back up photos and files first.

Are apps deleted during school hours?

No. Apps are switched off during the hours you choose and come back automatically afterwards, with their data kept.

How do payments work?

You subscribe from your SafeTab workspace and pay on Paystack's secure checkout. Your plan renews monthly. If a payment fails you get a 7-day grace period, and your data is never deleted because of a missed payment.

Which devices are supported?

Android tablets and phones that support Android Enterprise. iPads are not supported yet.
